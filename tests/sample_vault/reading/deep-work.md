# Deep Work — Cal Newport

#reading #productivity #books

## Core thesis
Cognitively demanding work done in a state of distraction-free concentration
(deep work) is becoming rare and increasingly valuable. The ability to quickly
master hard things and produce at an elite level are both consequences of deep work.

## Four rules
1. **Work deeply** — schedule it like a meeting, protect the block
2. **Embrace boredom** — stop reaching for your phone at every idle moment
3. **Quit social media** — or at least be intentional about it
4. **Drain the shallows** — time-box shallow tasks, don't let them expand

## My takeaway
The study blocks I protect on Tuesday and Thursday mornings are genuinely more
productive than scattered hours throughout the day. The book gives me language
for why: uninterrupted time lets working memory build up context that
context-switching destroys.

Keep protecting the morning blocks.
